Project Overview

What is this project about?

This project analyzes a Portuguese bank's telemarketing campaign to identify which customers are most likely to subscribe to a term deposit — and what the bank should do differently.

The Business Problem

The bank contacted 11,162 customers by phone to sell term deposit subscriptions. Only 47% subscribed. The remaining 53% represent wasted outreach effort, agent time, and campaign budget.

The goal of this analysis is to answer: Who subscribes, when, why, and how can the bank do better?

What I Built

A complete EDA pipeline in Python followed by a 3-page interactive Power BI dashboard with 18+ visuals, DAX measures, dynamic slicers, and navigation buttons — delivering insights across customer demographics, financial profile, and campaign strategy.

Strategic Action Plan

What should management do?

Six data-driven recommendations ranked by priority, expected impact, and the specific finding that supports each action.

1
Retarget previous campaign winners immediately
Identify all customers whose previous campaign outcome was "success" and contact them first — via cellular — in March, September, October or December. These customers convert at 91.3%, almost double the average rate.
🚨 Urgent
2
Cap all agents at 3 contact attempts per customer
Conversion drops from 53.4% at contact 1 to 25.0% at contact 8. Set a hard limit of 3 contacts per customer and reallocate the saved call time to reaching new prospects instead.
🚨 Urgent
3
Mandate cellular contact — eliminate unknown contact type entries
Cellular outperforms unknown contact type by 31.7 percentage points (54.3% vs 22.6%). Require agents to always log the contact method in the CRM. Unknown entries are costing the bank potential subscribers.
⚠️ High
4
Prioritize tertiary-educated, single, high-balance, debt-free customers
Stack the profile: tertiary education (+14.7%), single status (+9%), high balance (+23%), no housing loan (+20%). Customers matching all four criteria are significantly more likely to subscribe than the average customer.
⚠️ High
5
Schedule campaigns in peak conversion months only
March (89.9%), September (84.3%), October (82.4%), and December (90.9%) deliver conversion rates 2–3x higher than January or May. Restructure campaign calendars around these windows.
💡 Medium
6
Train agents to extend call quality — target 6+ minute conversations
Subscribers averaged 6.20 minutes per call vs much shorter calls for non-subscribers. Train agents on engagement techniques to keep customers on the line longer through genuine conversation, not pressure tactics.
💡 Medium
📈 Bottom line: Implementing actions 1–3 alone could realistically improve campaign ROI by 30–40% by focusing resources on the highest-converting segments and eliminating wasted contact attempts.
